Privacy & Terms

Last Updated: October 23, 2025

🔐 Our Privacy Promise

Your data is secure and private. We do not share, sell, or expose your personal information to any third parties.

  • Email & Passwords: Stored securely with Authentication - never shared.
  • Farm Data: Your poultry records remain private and encrypted.
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • Data Storage: Syncs only to your personal cloud account via Google Firebase.
  • Transparency: We collect minimal data required for app functionality.

📊 How Data is Used

We use the information collected to provide a seamless farming experience:

  • Authentication: To secure your account and identify your data.
  • Cloud Sync: To ensure your records are available across all your devices.
  • Performance: Anonymous usage statistics to fix bugs and improve tools.
  • Support: To assist you when you request help via WhatsApp or Email.

📋 Important Terms

Veterinary Consultation Required

Before administering vaccines or medications, always consult with a qualified veterinarian. This app is for record-keeping only.

We are not responsible for poultry deaths, illnesses, or financial losses. The app is a tool, not a substitute for professional management.

✅ What We DON'T Do

  • ❌ Never share your email with marketers.
  • ❌ Never sell your farm data to third parties.
  • ❌ Never read your personal messages or contacts.
  • ❌ Never track you across other apps or websites.
  • ❌ Never store passwords in plain text.
