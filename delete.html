Account Deletion Request

We are sorry to see you go. If you would like to delete your Poultry Tracker account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly within the app:

  1. Open the Poultry Tracker app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap on Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your password and follow the prompts to permanently erase your data.

Method 2: Email Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by sending an email to our support team. Please include your registered email address or User ID.

Request Account Deletion

Note: Once your request is processed, your account and all data associated with it will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
